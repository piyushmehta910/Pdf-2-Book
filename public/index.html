Welcome to the Book Studio

How a book is structured — every professional book flows: front cover → title page → copyright → dedication → table of contents → chapters → appendices/glossary/references → about the author.

  • Left panel — your sources, chapter outline, and knowledge graph.
  • Center canvas — live print-paginated preview of your chapters.
  • Right panel — AI refinement, provenance, search, and quality audit.

Getting started: drop PDFs, DOCX or notes into the Sources tab, press Extract All, then ✨ Write Book to generate a professionally structured book. Apply a design theme, page size, and export to PDF, EPUB, or Markdown.
